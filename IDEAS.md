# Ideas

## Cursor

- limited HP
- damage on enemy/projectile contact
- shoot projectiles

## Navigation

- `h`/`j`/`k`/`l`: move by 1
- `gh`/`gl`/`gg`/`ge`: move to end

## Enemies

- move around
- limited HP
- damage on enemy/projectile contact
- shoot projectiles
- when enemies die, transition `⠿ -> ⠷ -> ⠶ -> ⠦ -> ⠤ -> ⠄ -> ⠀`

## Projectiles

- `•`
- cancel out on contact

## Obstacles

- blocks projectiles
- blocks navigation

## Controls

- `q`: exit; save state
